The problem

Habit apps are free to quit

A streak costs nothing

Break it and nothing happens. After the first busy week, most people stop.

Points fade fast

Badges and points feel good on day one. By day ten they mean nothing.

Loss works, trust doesn't

People fight harder not to lose. Commitment apps use that, but a company holds the money.

What if missing a day cost you something real, and nobody could cheat?

PLEDGEX · 02

How it works

One pledge, three steps

01

Lock

Pick a habit, the days and a stake. Your SKR goes into an escrow nobody can touch, not even us.

02

Check in

Keep the day, then tap Check in. A key on your phone signs it. No wallet popup.

03

Settle

Kept days come back to you. Missed days are burned. Anyone can settle, and a bot does it hourly.

EXAMPLE

7 days · 1,500 SKR · keep 5, miss 2 →

1,071.43 back

428.57 burned

Integrity

What the chain enforces, and what it can't yet

Enforced on chain

Only SKR can be staked

One check-in per day, inside that day

6 AM and screen windows timed by Solana's clock
