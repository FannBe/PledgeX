PledgeX

Stake on your daily steps. Walk, and it comes back. Skip a day, and that day's stake burns: nobody receives it, not even us.

Download the Android app Source code

How it works

1 · Pledge

Choose a daily step goal, a number of days and a stake. The stake moves into an escrow account of the PledgeX program on Solana.

2 · Walk and clock in

When your phone's step counter reaches the goal, tap Clock in. A key kept on the phone signs it, so there is no wallet screen. That key can clock in and nothing else.

3 · Settle

After the last day, the program returns the days you kept and burns the days you missed. Anyone can trigger settle, but the refund only goes to you.

Try the whole thing in six minutes

  1. Install the app. Tap Use a demo wallet, or connect Phantom with Testnet Mode on (Settings → Developer Settings).
  2. Tap Get devnet SOL, then Get 10,000 test SKR. The test SKR comes from the program's own faucet.
  3. Create a pledge with Day length: Demo (2 minutes) and 3 days.
  4. On day 1, tap Add 1,000 simulated steps until you reach the goal, then Clock in. Let day 2 pass without clocking in. Clock in on day 3.
  5. After six minutes, tap Settle. Two thirds of the stake come back and one third is burned. Every step shows up in On-chain activity with a Solana Explorer link.

Simulated steps are only available in demo pledges and are labelled as simulated. A real pledge uses the phone's hardware step counter and 24-hour days.

Honest limits

The phone reports the steps

The program checks who clocks in, which day it is and that each day counts once. It cannot check the step count itself: that number comes from the phone.

Devnet and test tokens

PledgeX runs on Solana devnet. Test SKR has no value and is not the real SKR token.
