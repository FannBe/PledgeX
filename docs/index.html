How it works

Three steps, all on chain

Each step is a real Solana transaction you can open on Explorer.

01 · PLEDGE

Lock a stake

Choose a habit, a number of days and a stake. create_commitment moves the stake into a vault owned by the PledgeX program. No team key can touch it.

02 · CLOCK IN

One tap a day

Keep the habit, then tap Clock in. A key kept on your phone signs clock_in, so there is no wallet screen each morning. That key can clock in and nothing else.

03 · SETTLE

Kept days back, missed days burned

After the last day anyone can call settle. Kept days return to you, missed days are destroyed with SPL burn, and the accounts close with their rent sent back to you.

Habits

Habits the program enforces

The program refuses a clock-in outside the window, below the goal, over the limit, twice in one day, or signed by any other key.

Steps

3,000 to 10,000 steps a day, counted by the phone's hardware step counter. Clock in any time once you reach the goal.

The 6:00 AM Club

Clock in between 05:00 and 06:00 local time. Solana's clock alone decides: the program accepts a clock-in only in the first hour of the day, so this one cannot be faked.

Screen detox

Stay under 1, 2 or 3 hours of screen time, read from Android's usage statistics. Clock in between 22:00 and midnight, once the day's total is known.

The app

Built for the Seeker

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on a pitch-black OLED theme in Solana purple and mint. Four tabs and a Judge Lab.

Two ways in

Connect Phantom or Solflare through Mobile Wallet Adapter, or use a demo wallet kept on the phone. Both send the same real transactions.

Soulbound badges

Six badges (First Pledge, 10K Pioneer, 6 AM Club Hero, Digital Detox, Week Streak, Iron Will), read from your on-chain Profile: a program account that cannot be sold or transferred.

Ranks from the chain

A leaderboard by days kept, total staked and burned, the success rate and the latest program transactions, all read from devnet when the tab opens.

Reminders & sharing

A notification before today's window closes (or when the 6 AM window opens), and a result card to share with its Explorer link.

For judges

The whole thing in six minutes

Demo pledges use 2-minute days, so a 3-day pledge lives its whole life while you watch.

  1. Install the APK from the latest release. Tap Use a demo wallet, or Connect wallet with Phantom in Testnet Mode (Settings → Developer Settings).
  2. Open Judge Lab in the header. Tap Get devnet SOL and Get 10,000 test SKR. The test SKR is minted by the program's own faucet.
  3. Tap Lock demo pledge: 3,000 steps a day, 1,000 test SKR, three 2-minute days.
  4. On day 1, walk or tap +1,000 simulated steps (demo only, labelled). Then tap Clock in. There is no wallet screen, because the session key signs.
  5. Let day 2 pass, then clock in on day 3. After six minutes tap Settle & claim: two thirds come back and one third burns. Check Vault and Ranks.

Also try the 6 AM Club in demo mode. A clock-in is accepted only in the first 40 seconds of each 2-minute day; after that the chain refuses it.

Trust

Security and honest limits

What protects your stake

The vault is a program account, so no team key can withdraw it. The session key can only call clock_in, settle pays only the owner, and burned tokens go to nobody. The upgrade authority is held offline, and the old development key that leaked had every authority removed before launch.

What the program cannot check

Steps and screen time are reported by the phone. The program checks who clocks in, when and how often, not the number itself. Only the 6 AM Club is verified entirely on chain. PledgeX runs on devnet with a test token that is not the real SKR.
